# Standard Product Catalog (Non-Customizable)

A browsable shop displaying our pre-designed, ready-to-ship pieces. This is the conventional e-commerce half of the site; it should feel polished but is not the differentiator — the [custom designer](./04-designer-philosophy.md) is.

## Browse & discovery

- **Categories** by occasion (baby showers, weddings, religious holidays, corporate, etc.) and by piece type (name plaque, monogram, frame, companion shape).
- **Filters**: occasion, size, finish, color, price.
- **Search**: full-text search across product titles, descriptions, and tags.
- **Sort**: relevance, price (asc/desc), newest, best-selling.

## Product detail page

- Image gallery with **zoom**.
- Multi-angle photography (front, side showing thickness, mounted on stand).
- Size, finish, material breakdown.
- Reviews and rating.
- **Related products** section.
- Add to cart / wishlist actions.
- Care instructions (or link to [content page](./19-seo-and-content.md)).

## Inventory

- Per-product inventory tracking.
- "Out of stock" handling — show clearly, disable add-to-cart.
- **Back-in-stock notifications** — customer email signup; trigger when restocked.

## Wishlist / favorites

- Logged-in customers can favorite products.
- Persisted to their account.
- Wishlists viewable from the customer account dashboard.

## Open questions

- Do we want a public "all products" page, or only category-driven browsing?
- Should reviews be moderated before publishing, or trust-and-flag?
- Do we surface "made to order vs in stock" as a filter, or is the catalog strictly stocked items?
