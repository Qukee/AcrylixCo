# Business Context

## What AcrylixCo is

A custom acrylic engraving and laser-cut decor business based in Sydney, Australia. We produce decorative and commemorative acrylic pieces for life events and occasions:

- **Personal milestones**: baby showers, birthdays, weddings, anniversaries
- **Public holidays**: Christmas, Easter, Mother's / Father's Day, Valentine's Day
- **Religious holidays**: Eid / Ramadan, Diwali, Hanukkah, Christmas
- **Corporate**: gifts, awards, branded pieces
- **Home**: personalized decor

## Signature look

Multi-layered laser-cut acrylic. The defining construction:

- A **foreground** piece — usually text or a main decorative shape
- Bonded on top of a slightly larger **base** piece
- The base is cut to follow the foreground's contour with an outward offset
- This produces a **halo of color** around the design

Pieces span a range of finishes (mirror, matte, frosted, gloss, glitter, neon) and color palettes, with mounted stands for tabletop display.

## What this site has to do

Two distinct jobs:

1. Sell our pre-made catalog (a fairly conventional e-commerce flow).
2. Let customers compose their own custom pieces.

The custom designer is the differentiator. It is **not a "text personalization" widget** — it is a layered acrylic composition builder. The right mental model is "simplified Figma / Cricut Design Space," not "name on a mug."

## Market specifics

- **Australian-first**: GST handling, Australia Post shipping, Afterpay support are non-negotiable.
- **Mobile-heavy**: Australian customers shop on phones; the designer must work cleanly on phones and tablets.
- **Multi-cultural customer base**: multi-script support (Latin, Chinese, Arabic, Cyrillic at minimum) is required, not optional.
- **Premium price point**: the design preview must be high-quality enough to justify the price and reduce post-purchase complaints.

## Constraints I (the owner) am working under

- I am a non-developer. Day-to-day product, order, template, and finish-library operations must be doable through an admin UI without touching code.
- I want the codebase typed, tested where it matters (especially the offset-outline geometry), and documented enough that a future hired developer can extend it.
